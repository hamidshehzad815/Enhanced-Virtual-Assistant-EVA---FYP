CLF_Improvement Guide (CLF1 + CLF2)
==================================

Goal
----
Improve EVA's two classifiers so they handle:
- ambiguous "show" messages
- behavioral vs scheduling confusion
- multi-command vs single command
- confirmations ("yes", "no") vs commands
- casual general questions vs commands

CLF1 (Input type) labels
-----------------------
- command
- general
- out_of_scope
- confirmation
- multiple_command

CLF2 (Intent) labels (router-supported)
--------------------------------------
- scheduling_add
- scheduling_delete
- scheduling_update
- scheduling_show
- learning_create
- learning_update
- learning_delete
- learning_show
- behavioral

Key best practices (high impact)
--------------------------------
1) Hard negatives for "show"
   - scheduling_show: show/list/calendar/tasks/today/tomorrow/this week + task nouns
   - behavioral: "behavioral summary", "my habits", "weekly insights", "productivity report"
   - learning_show: "show my roadmap", "progress in python", "learning progress", "study plan status"

2) Multi-command realism (CLF1 multiple_command)
   - Use conjunctions: "and", "then", commas, semicolons
   - Mix domains: "add meeting ... and show my behavioral summary"
   - Include polite wrappers: "please", "can you", "hey eva"

3) Confirmation realism (CLF1 confirmation)
   - Short: "yes", "no", "yep", "nah"
   - With context words: "yes do it", "no keep it", "confirm", "cancel it"
   Note: In production, confirmation is most common when Redis is waiting for yes/no.

4) General vs command boundary (CLF1)
   - general: "what can you do?", "how does scheduling work?"
   - command: "show my tasks", "add exam on friday"

5) Coverage
   - Include typos, casing, punctuation, emojis (optional), Urdu/roman-urdu snippets if you expect them.
   - Include very short commands ("show", "delete", "update") + more specific ones.

Data generation approach
------------------------
Templates live in:
  templates/clf1/*.json
  templates/clf2/*.json

Generators live in:
  generators/build_dataset.py
  generators/balance_dataset.py
  generators/validate_dataset.py

Output datasets:
  datasets/clf1/final/clf1.jsonl
  datasets/clf2/final/clf2.jsonl

Training (Colab)
----------------
Use:
  training/colab/Train_CLF1.ipynb
  training/colab/Train_CLF2.ipynb

Train one at a time, download final model zip, and deploy:
  CLF1 -> Eva_AI_Server/models/clf1/
  CLF2 -> Eva_AI_Server/models/intent_classifier/

After deploy
------------
Restart EVA AI Server.
Test the exact confusion cases:
- "show physics exam task" (scheduling_show)
- "show my behavioral summary" (behavioral)
- "show my learning progress in python" (learning_show)
